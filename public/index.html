CuminDB Genomic Repository

CuminDB is a comprehensive computational genomics portal for Cuminum cyminum L. (Cumin; Family Apiaceae, 2n = 14, ~2.6 Gb genome size), an economically vital spice and medicinal crop prized globally for its essential oils, bioactive monoterpenes (notably cuminaldehyde), and therapeutic properties. CuminDB compiles draft genome assembly annotations, 33,595 structural gene models mapped with OmicsBox/Blast2GO functional annotations, 213,248 SSR microsatellite loci with thermodynamic PCR primers, 1,065 plant transcription factor models across 53 families, 887,912 psRNATarget microRNA target interactions cross-referenced with official miRBase accessions, and secondary metabolite biosynthetic gene clusters. Interactive tools include an embedded JBrowse 2 linear genome browser and a local SequenceServer BLAST alignment engine to facilitate molecular breeding, marker-assisted selection, and comparative genomics across Apiaceae species.

Species Overview & Phytochemical Profile

  • Taxonomy & Origin: Cuminum cyminum L. ("Jeera"/"Zeera") is an annual medicinal spice crop in the Apiaceae family (Umbelliferae), originating in the Mediterranean & Middle East.
  • Global Export Share: India is the world's largest exporter of cumin (62.83% global share), with Rajasthan and Gujarat contributing 80% of national production.
  • Botanical Features: Slender branched stems (30–50 cm), compound leaves, pink umbel flowers, and cremocarp fruits containing aromatic seeds.
  • Phytochemical Composition: Seeds are rich in cuminaldehyde (~63% of total essential oil), monoterpenes, flavonoids, and essential minerals (Cu, Fe, K) & vitamins (B, C, E).
  • Therapeutic Value: Exhibits potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, antidiabetic, carminative, antiflatulent, and immunomodulatory properties.

Genome Architecture & CuminDB Scope

  • Genome Size & Karyotype: Diploid genome (2n = 14; 2.66 pg / ~2,601 Mb) with 157.38 kb chloroplast and 241.72 kb mitochondrial organelle genomes.
  • Repeat Complexity: High proportion of long, near-identical repeats (~70–80% transposable elements), representing a major genomic assembly challenge.
  • Cultivar Focus: First-ever draft genome sequence platform constructed for the Indian cultivar GC-20 (developed by SDAU, Gujarat).
  • Integrated Genomics Scope: Compiles 33,595 structural genes, 213,248 SSR markers with PCR primers, 1,065 plant TFs, 887,912 miRNA targets, and secondary metabolite clusters.
  • Omics Tools: Features embedded JBrowse 2 genome browser and local SequenceServer BLAST engines for molecular breeding and Apiaceae comparative genomics.

SequenceServer Web BLAST Search Engine Architecture

NCBI BLAST+ v2.14.0+ Integrated
E-value Threshold: 1e-5 Real-Time FASTA Auto-Detection

The CuminDB Web BLAST Search Engine is powered by an integrated local SequenceServer alignment daemon. It enables rapid, high-throughput sequence similarity searches across Cuminum cyminum genomic scaffolds, predicted coding sequences (CDS), and translated protein models using ncbi-blast+ indexes with interactive circular chord and ribbon visualization graphics.

Nucleotide Database Indexing -dbtype nucl
makeblastdb -in cumin_predicted_cds.fasta -dbtype nucl -title "Cumin_CDS"

Indexes 33,595 high-confidence coding sequences for blastn and tblastn searches.

Protein Database Indexing -dbtype prot
makeblastdb -in cumin_predicted_protein.fasta -dbtype prot -title "Cumin_Protein"

Indexes 33,595 translated amino acid sequence models for blastp and blastx searches.

CuminDB User Guide & Bioinformatics Tutorial

Welcome to the CuminDB Portal Tutorial. This comprehensive guide provides step-by-step instructions for navigating structural gene models, functional annotations, SSR microsatellites, transcription factors, miRNA target networks, Web BLAST alignments, and JBrowse 2 linear genome tracks for Cuminum cyminum.

1. Platform Architecture & Data Pipeline
System Overview

CuminDB integrates computational genomics and multi-omics annotation datasets generated for Cuminum cyminum (Cumin). The portal enables rapid locus lookup, comparative ortholog mapping, primer mining, and sequence alignment visualization.

1
Genome Assembly & Models
147,524 contigs reference assembly containing 30,619 predicted gene structural models (GFF3).
  • Contig scaffold coordinates
  • Exon/CDS structural boundaries
  • Tabix indexed GFF3 tracks
2
Functional Annotations
OmicsBox / Blast2GO functional assignments cross-referenced with international biological databases.
  • AmiGO 2 Gene Ontology (GO) IDs
  • ExPASy ENZYME (EC) classification
  • InterPro signature domains
3
Markers & Regulation
Mined markers and regulatory gene networks for marker-assisted breeding and functional discovery.
  • 213,248 SSR PCR primer pairs
  • 1,049 TFs across 44 families
  • 887,911 miRNA target interactions
Pro Tip: Quick Navigation & Exporting
Every data table in CuminDB supports instant search filtering, adjustable pagination rows (10, 20, 50, 100), and one-click CSV export via the top-right Export CSV button.
2. Querying Gene Models & Functional Annotations
Annotation Portal

Access the Annotation → Functional Annotation tab to search through predicted gene models and detailed functional ontology assignments.

1
Flexible Keyword Search
Type standardized identifiers or functional terms into the search bar:
  • Gene ID: CcGene_00001
  • Contig Locus: CcContig_000001
  • GO Term: GO:0009409
  • EC Number: EC 2.7.11.1
2
Interactive Badge Links
Directly inspect external bioinformatics databases via color-coded badges:
  • GO IDs → Opens AmiGO 2 database
  • EC Codes → Opens ExPASy ENZYME
  • InterPro → Opens EBI InterPro
3
Gene Details Modal
Click the View button on any row or click the Gene ID hyperlink to open the full annotation modal containing exact genomic coordinates, full GO term descriptions, and InterPro domain names.
3. Secondary Metabolite Biosynthetic Pathways
Metabolism Mining

Navigate to Annotation → Secondary Metabolism to explore 294 curated secondary metabolite pathway genes essential for cumin essential oils, aroma, and defense mechanisms.

1
Filter by Category
Select specific metabolic classes from the Pathway Category dropdown menu:
  • Terpenoids: Essential oil biosynthesis (cuminaldehyde, pinene, terpene synthases)
  • Phenylpropanoids: Monoterpenes & flavonoids
  • Alkaloids & Polyketides: Defense compounds
2
KEGG Pathway Diagrams
Click hyperlinked KEGG Map IDs (e.g., map00900) to launch official Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes interactive pathway diagrams in a new browser tab.
4. Mining SSR Microsatellites & PCR Primers
Molecular Breeding

Access the SSR tab to query 213,248 simple sequence repeat (SSR) loci mined across contigs and predicted gene regions, complete with pre-designed PCR primer pairs.

1
Motif & Type Filtering
Filter microsatellites by repeat length (Dinucleotide, Trinucleotide, etc.) or query specific nucleotide motifs (e.g., GA, CTT).
  • Dinucleotide (e.g., AT, GA, CT)
  • Trinucleotide (e.g., CTT, GAA)
  • Tetra/Penta/Hexanucleotide
2
Primer Specifications & Copy
Each marker includes Primer3 designed parameters:
  • Forward & Reverse primer sequences (5' → 3')
  • Melting Temperatures (Tm in °C)
  • Expected amplicon product size (bp)
5. Transcription Factors & TAIR Orthologs
Gene Regulation

Access the TFs tab to inspect 1,049 predicted transcription factor genes classified into 44 major plant TF families.

1
TF Family Classification
Filter by major regulatory families such as MYB, bHLH, AP2/ERF, NAC, bZIP, and WRKY using the TF Family dropdown.
2
Arabidopsis TAIR Links
View best-hit Arabidopsis thaliana locus IDs, BLAST alignment E-values, and click TAIR Accession hyperlinks to study functional orthology.
6. microRNA Target Networks & miRBase Integration
Small RNA Regulation

Access the miRNA tab to explore 887,911 plant microRNA target interactions cross-referenced with miRBase accessions.

1
Target Interaction Metrics
Query by microRNA accession (e.g., ath-miR156a) or target gene ID:
  • Expectation Score (lower indicates tighter binding)
  • Mechanism: Cleavage vs Translational Inhibition
  • Duplex alignment string representation
2
miRBase Database Links
Click hyperlinked miRBase ID tags to open official miRBase precursor hairpin structures and sequence mature arm annotations.
7. SequenceServer Web BLAST & JBrowse 2 Tracks
Alignment & Visualization

Navigate to Tools → BLAST or Tools → JBrowse to run alignment searches or visualize linear genome browser tracks.

1
Executing Web BLAST
Paste nucleotide or protein FASTA sequences into the query box. Select alignment parameters:
  • Programs: blastn, blastp, blastx
  • Databases: Cumin CDS, Cumin Protein, Assembly Contigs
  • Expect Threshold: Default 1e-5
2
JBrowse 2 Linear Genome View
In BLAST search results, click View in JBrowse 2 to jump straight to the exact contig coordinates and view GFF3 gene tracks in the embedded JBrowse 2 viewer.

8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
User Guide
How do I cite CuminDB in my research publication? ▼

If you utilize datasets, PCR primers, annotation models, or BLAST results from CuminDB, please cite:

Ajay Kumar Mahato, Lakshmi Devi, Priyanka Kushwaha, Ramesh Eerapagula, Ankit Bhagat. CuminDB: An Interactive Genomic and Functional Annotation Database for Cuminum cyminum L. Scientific Data (2026).
What bioinformatics pipelines were used to generate the annotations? ▼
Functional gene ontology assignments were performed using OmicsBox / Blast2GO against non-redundant plant databases. SSR microsatellites and PCR primers were mined using MISA and Primer3. Transcription factors were classified using iTAK / PlantTFDB standards, and microRNA targets were identified using psRNATarget mapped against miRBase v22.
Can I download full FASTA files or bulk CSV tables? ▼
Yes! Navigate to the Downloads tab in the navigation bar to download complete genome assembly FASTA files, predicted CDS/protein sequences, full GFF3 gene annotations, SSR primer tables, TF families, and miRNA targets in CSV format.
How do I search for SSR primers for a specific gene? ▼
Navigate to the SSR tab and type the Gene ID (e.g. CcGene_00005) into the search bar. The table will display all microsatellite loci intersecting that gene model, complete with forward/reverse primer sequences, Tm temperatures, and product sizes.
Who maintains CuminDB and how can I submit questions or feedback? ▼
CuminDB is developed and maintained by the Laboratory of Genome Informatics (LGI) at the BRIC - Centre for DNA Fingerprinting and Diagnostics (CDFD), Hyderabad, India. For inquiries or collaboration, contact Dr. Ajay Kumar Mahato at lgi@cdfd.org.in or view the Contact tab.
